# MP3 Player

Grabs songs from YouTube, converts them to best-quality MP3s with the video
thumbnail as cover art, and copies them onto your MP3 player. Also lists what
is on the player and lets you delete tracks.

Paste a YouTube link, press Add, then confirm. The player folder defaults to
`/Volumes/Untitled/music`; use Change in the list footer to point at another
folder. The choice is saved on this machine.
